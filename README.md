# What is haunted door ?
> it's really a single scarry looking door only which u shouldn;t open but obviously you will :) heh heh :>
The idea of haunted door  is pretty simple. You enter a dark hallway and see a creepy old door. The clock starts ticking at 11:59:45 PM, and you have 15 seconds before midnight.
At 12:00 AM, things change.

A flashlight appears, the door becomes interactable, and well... you know what happens next. (hehe can't tell yayy u have to see for your self  you have to wait and see after the door open to see the suprise)

# Feature 
> it doesn't that many feature but below are some which u will see in it
- interactive door
- mouse-controlled flashlight
- volume controler
